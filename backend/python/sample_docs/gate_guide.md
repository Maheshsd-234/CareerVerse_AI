# GATE 2025: Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering

## What is GATE?

**GATE (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering)** is a national-level exam jointly administered by the **IITs and IISc** for:
1. Admission to **M.Tech / M.E. / M.S. programs** at IITs, NITs, and top universities.
2. **PSU Recruitment** — BHEL, ONGC, IOCL, NTPC, BARC, etc. use GATE scores for direct hiring.
3. GATE score is valid for **3 years**.

## GATE Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme

- **65 questions, 100 marks, 3 hours**.
- 25 questions from General Aptitude (15 marks) + 55 questions from the chosen paper (85 marks).
- Question types: MCQ (1 or 2 marks) + Numerical Answer Type (NAT, no negative marking).
- **Negative marking:** -1/3 for 1-mark MCQs; -2/3 for 2-mark MCQs. NAT questions have no negative marking.

## Preparation Strategy

### For M.Tech Admission

- Aim for **GATE score 600+** and **AIR under 1000** for IIT admission (CS).
- Focus on core subjects: DS&A, OS, DBMS, COA, CN for CS.
- Standard references: CLRS (Algorithms), Galvin (OS), Korth (DBMS), Forouzan (CN).
- Practice standard GATE PYQs from 2010–present (available free on GATE overflow and GO Book).

### For PSU Recruitment

- Different PSUs have different cut-offs — BARC typically needs AIR < 500 (EE/EC).
- IOCL, ONGC, HPCL accept GATE scores and conduct separate interviews.
- BHEL generally accepts GATE score + written test + interview.

## M.Tech vs PSU: Which to Choose?

- **M.Tech at IIT:** Better for R&D, academia, FAANG-type product roles post degree. Takes 2 years.
- **PSU Job:** Immediate income, job security, government benefits. Good for those with family responsibilities.
- Some students do both — get PSU offer, pursue M.Tech, and resign if better opportunity arises.
