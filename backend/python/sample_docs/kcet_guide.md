# KCET 2025: Karnataka Common Entrance Test Guide

## What is KCET?

**KCET (Karnataka Common Entrance Test)** is conducted by the **Karnataka Examination Authority (KEA)** for admission to Engineering, Pharmacy, and B.Sc. Agriculture programs in Karnataka government and private colleges. It is exclusively for Karnataka domicile students.

## Eligibility

- Must be a Karnataka resident (Category 1: Horanadu/Gadinadu Kannadigas also eligible).
- Passed/appearing in Class 12 (PUC II) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology.
- No minimum percentage cut-off for appearing; however, college-specific cut-offs apply.
- Age: No upper age limit.

## KCET Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme

| Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---------|-----------|-------|----------|
| Mathematics | 60 | 60 | 80 min |
| Physics | 60 | 60 | 80 min |
| Chemistry | 60 | 60 | 80 min |
| Biology (optional — for Pharmacy/Agri) | 60 | 60 | 80 min |

- **No negative marking.**
- Questions are based on Karnataka PUC syllabus (NCERT equivalent but slightly different).
- Kannada Language Test (KLT) is mandatory for Category 1 students.

## KCET Rank Calculation

KCET merit list is based on:
- **50% weightage** to KCET score.
- **50% weightage** to PUC (Class 12) marks in the relevant subjects.

This means your board marks are equally important — do not neglect PUC exams!

## Preparation Tips

### Subject-wise Strategy

- **Mathematics:** Practice all Karnataka PUC textbook problems + previous year KCET papers.
- **Physics:** Focus on laws, formulas, and numerical problems. NCERT is the base.
- **Chemistry:** Organic, Inorganic, and Physical — Karnataka PUC textbook first, then NCERT.
- **No negative marking** → attempt all 60 questions in each subject.

### Books & Resources

- Karnataka PUC textbooks (I and II year) — primary resource.
- KCET Explorer (CET guide) by Target Publications.
- Previous 10 years KCET question papers (available on KEA website).

## Fees

- **Government Engineering Colleges:** ₹25,000–₹50,000 per year.
- **Private Colleges (KCET seat):** ₹70,000–₹1.5 lakhs per year.
- **NRI/Management Quota:** ₹4–12 lakhs per year (not covered by KCET).
