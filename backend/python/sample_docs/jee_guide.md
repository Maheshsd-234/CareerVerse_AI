# JEE Mains & Advanced: Complete Guide for Indian Students

## What is JEE?

The **Joint Entrance Examination (JEE)** is the national-level entrance test for admission to **IITs, NITs, IIITs**, and other centrally funded technical institutes across India. It has two stages:

- **JEE Mains** — conducted by NTA (National Testing Agency), 2 sessions per year (January & April).
- **JEE Advanced** — conducted by rotating IITs; only the top ~2.5 lakh JEE Mains qualifiers can appear.

## Eligibility Criteria

- Must have passed Class 12 (or equivalent) with **Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics**.
- Minimum **75% aggregate** (65% for SC/ST) in Class 12 boards. Top 20 percentile in board also accepted.
- Maximum **3 attempts** for JEE Mains, **2 attempts** for JEE Advanced.
- Age limit: Not more than 25 years (30 for SC/ST/PwD) as of October 1 of the exam year.

## JEE Mains Exam Pattern

| Section | Questions | Marks | Negative Marking |
|---------|-----------|-------|-----------------|
| Physics | 20 MCQ + 5 Integer | 100 | -1 for MCQ; none for Integer |
| Chemistry | 20 MCQ + 5 Integer | 100 | -1 for MCQ; none for Integer |
| Mathematics | 20 MCQ + 5 Integer | 100 | -1 for MCQ; none for Integer |

**Total: 90 questions, 300 marks, 3 hours**

## JEE Advanced Exam Pattern

JEE Advanced has Paper 1 and Paper 2, each of 3 hours. Combined marks vary by year (~360 marks total). Question types include MCQ (single/multiple correct), integer, and match-the-column.

## Preparation Strategy

### Class 11 Foundation

- Complete NCERT thoroughly for all three subjects.
- Start with H.C. Verma (Physics), Morrison & Boyd (Organic Chemistry), and Coordinate Geometry by S.L. Loney.
- Join a reputed coaching (FIITJEE, Allen, Resonance, Aakash) or credible online platform (Vedantu, Unacademy, PW).

### Class 12 + Drop Year

- Solve previous year papers (PYPs) from 2010 onwards.
- Mock tests every weekend.
- Focus on weak chapters instead of revising strong ones repeatedly.
- Aim for 160+ in Mains and 180+ in Advanced to secure a good IIT branch.

## Salary After IIT (Approximate)

- **Campus placement (India):** ₹12–40 LPA for CS/EE (varies by company and IIT).
- **FAANG/product companies:** ₹40–100+ LPA for IIT CS graduates.
- **Core engineering roles:** ₹6–18 LPA for non-CS streams.

## Tips from Toppers

1. Consistency beats intensity — 6 focused hours daily beats 14 distracted hours.
2. Do not skip revision. A topic not revised in 3 weeks is nearly forgotten.
3. Error logs: maintain a notebook of mistakes made in tests.
4. Sleep 7–8 hours — memory consolidation is crucial for problem-solving.
