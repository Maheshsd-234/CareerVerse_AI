## By Company Type

### IT Services (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL)

- **Fresher CTC:** ₹3.5–7 LPA.
- Annual increments: 8–12%.
- Growth path: Developer → Analyst → Senior Analyst → Manager (5–8 years to ₹15–20 LPA).
- Note: On-site (US/Europe) opportunities can multiply take-home pay significantly.

### Indian Product Companies (Razorpay, CRED, PhonePe, Swiggy)

- **Fresher CTC:** ₹15–35 LPA.
- Fast growth — 18 months to double salary with job switches is common.
- ESOPs can significantly increase total compensation.

### MNC India Offices (Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Adobe)

- **Fresher SDE-1 CTC:** ₹20–45 LPA (base + RSU + bonus).
- RSUs vest over 4 years — add ₹5–20 LPA to annual comp at current stock prices.
- Senior SDE-2: ₹40–80 LPA; Staff/Principal: ₹80–150+ LPA.

### Startups (Series A–C)

- Higher risk, higher upside (ESOPs).
- Fresher: ₹10–25 LPA cash + ESOPs.
- If startup succeeds and IPOs, ESOPs can be life-changing.

## Salary Negotiation Tips

1. **Always negotiate** — first offers are rarely the best offers.
2. Know your **market rate**: use Glassdoor, Levels.fyi India, AmbitionBox, LinkedIn Salary.
3. **RSU/ESOPs** are not liquid — discount them 30–50% in your mental calculation.
4. **Total cost to company (CTC) vs in-hand:** Indian CTC includes PF (employer contribution), gratuity, and benefits that reduce actual take-home.
5. For fresher negotiations: focus on joining bonus and immediate variable pay, not just fixed.

## Cost of Living Impact

In Bengaluru / Mumbai, even ₹15 LPA can feel tight due to rent (₹15,000–₹35,000/month for 1BHK). In Tier-2 cities (Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai), ₹12 LPA can give a comfortable lifestyle.

Take-home salary (approximate) from annual CTC:
- ₹10 LPA CTC → ₹65,000–₹72,000/month in-hand.
- ₹20 LPA CTC → ₹1.3–1.45 lakhs/month in-hand.
- ₹40 LPA CTC → ₹2.5–2.8 lakhs/month in-hand.
- ₹80 LPA CTC → ₹4.5–5.2 lakhs/month in-hand.

(Calculations assume HRA exemption, standard deductions, new tax regime.)
