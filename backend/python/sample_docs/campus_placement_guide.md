# Campus Placement Guide: How to Get Hired Before Graduation

## What Is Campus Placement?

Campus placement is the recruitment process where companies visit engineering or management colleges to hire final-year students before graduation. Most B.Tech placements happen between August and February of the final year.

## Types of Campus Recruitment

### On-Campus (Campus Drive)

- Companies formally visit your college.
- Only your batch and sometimes previous years' unplaced students are eligible.
- Eligibility: typically a minimum CGPA (6.0–7.0 cut-off depending on company).
- No backlogs: Most service companies allow 0 active backlogs; product companies prefer 0 total.

### Off-Campus (External Drives)

- Apply directly to companies via their career portals or hiring platforms.
- Many companies run "off-campus drives" for students from all colleges.
- Platforms: LinkedIn, Naukri Campus, Unstop, HiringStudio, CoCubes.

### Pool Campus / Consortia

- Multiple colleges pool their students for one company drive.
- Common for IT service companies (TCS iON, Infosys InfyTQ, Wipro).

## The Placement Process

1. **Pre-Placement Talk (PPT):** Company introduces itself, role, and salary. Attend even if not interested — shows respect and you may be surprised.
2. **Aptitude Test:** Logical reasoning, quantitative aptitude, verbal ability, and often coding (30–90 min).
3. **Technical Interview(s):** 1–3 rounds focusing on DSA, DBMS, OS, projects (for SWE roles).
4. **HR Interview:** Cultural fit, career goals, salary expectations.
5. **Offer Letter:** Arrive within days to a few weeks after final round.

## DSA Preparation for Placement

For software engineering roles (both service and product companies), DSA is unavoidable.

### Minimum Bar

- **Arrays, Strings, Linked Lists, Stacks & Queues** — must be strong.
- **Binary Search, Sorting algorithms.**
- **Recursion, Backtracking.**
- **Basic Graphs (BFS, DFS), Trees (traversals, BST).**
- **Dynamic Programming** — at least easy/medium Fibonacci variants.

### Resources

- LeetCode 150 (NeetCode roadmap) — complete before placements.
- GeeksforGeeks Company-wise questions (filter by company).
- InterviewBit for structured roadmap.
- Striver's A2Z DSA Sheet (free on takeUforward.com) — comprehensive.

### Mock Interviews

- Pramp.com (free peer mock interviews).
- InterviewKickstart, Exponent for structured preparation.
- Practice with friends — explain your thought process out loud.

## Resume Tips for Final Year Students

- **One page only.**
- Sections: Contact → Education → Skills → Projects → Internships → Achievements/Extra.
- **Projects** are the most important for freshers without industry experience.
- Each project: 2–3 bullet points with impact metrics. "Built X using Y, resulting in Z."
- No typos. Use readable font (Calibri 11, Times New Roman 11). No photos.
- Use ATS-friendly format — avoid complex tables and columns.

## Projects That Impress Recruiters

| Project Type | Technologies | Why It Works |
|---|---|---|
| Full-stack web app | React + Node/Django + PostgreSQL | Demonstrates end-to-end skills |
| ML model with deployment | Python + Flask/FastAPI + Streamlit/Heroku | Shows both ML + deployment |
| Mobile app | Flutter / React Native | Growing demand, fewer applicants |
| Open source contribution | Any large GitHub project | Shows collaboration |
| Competitive Programming rating | Codeforces / LeetCode | Signal of problem-solving |

## CGPA vs Skills: Which Matters More?

- **Service companies (TCS, Infosys):** CGPA 6.0+ minimum; aptitude test is the real filter.
- **Mid-tier product (Freshworks, Razorpay):** Skills and projects matter more than CGPA (7.0+ helpful).
- **Top product (Google, Microsoft India):** DSA + system design + your GitHub > CGPA.
- **General principle:** CGPA below 7 can be compensated by strong projects and internships.

## Internships: The Biggest Multiplier

An internship at a good company → PPO (Pre-Placement Offer) in final year is the safest placement strategy:
- Apply for internships in Year 2 (Summer after 2nd year).
- Platforms: Internshala, LinkedIn, AngelList, company career portals.
- Even unpaid startups can give great projects and references.
