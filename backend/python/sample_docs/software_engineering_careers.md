# Software Engineering Careers in India: Roles & Salaries

## Overview

Software engineering is the largest employer of engineering graduates in India. The Indian IT industry generates over $230 billion in revenue (FY 2024) and employs 5+ million professionals. From service companies (TCS, Infosys) to product startups (Zepto, CRED), opportunities span all skill levels.

## Key Role Categories

### 1. Software Development Engineer (SDE)

The most common entry-level role. Build, test, and maintain software products.

**Skills required:**
- Data Structures & Algorithms (must for product company interviews).
- At least one programming language (Python, Java, C++, JavaScript).
- System Design (for senior levels — SDE-2 and above).
- Version control (Git), CI/CD basics.

**Salary (India, approximate):**
| Level | Service Cos. | Mid-stage Startups | Product Cos. (FAANG-type) |
|-------|-------------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| Fresher / SDE-1 | ₹3.5–8 LPA | ₹8–20 LPA | ₹20–45 LPA |
| SDE-2 (2–5 years) | ₹8–18 LPA | ₹18–35 LPA | ₹35–70 LPA |
| Senior SDE-3 (5–8 years) | ₹15–30 LPA | ₹30–60 LPA | ₹60–120 LPA |

### 2. Data Scientist / ML Engineer

Work with large datasets; build machine learning models for prediction, recommendation, and automation.

**Skills required:**
- Python (pandas, scikit-learn, PyTorch or TensorFlow).
- Statistics & probability.
- SQL and data wrangling.
- Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS SageMaker, GCP Vertex AI).
- Bonus: LLMs, RAG, prompt engineering (2025 most sought-after).

**Salary:**
- Freshers (B.Tech/M.Sc.): ₹8–18 LPA.
- Experienced (2–4 years): ₹18–40 LPA.
- Senior/Lead ML at product companies: ₹40–90 LPA.

### 3. DevOps / Cloud Engineer

Manage infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, Kubernetes clusters, and cloud services.

**Key tools:** Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, Jenkins, AWS/GCP/Azure.
**Salary:** ₹8–35 LPA depending on experience and certifications.

### 4. Frontend / Backend / Full Stack Engineer

- **Frontend:** React, Next.js, TypeScript, CSS.
- **Backend:** Node.js, Django, FastAPI, Spring Boot.
- **Full Stack:** Both. Highly employable at startups.
- Salary: ₹6–40 LPA (highly variable with company type).

### 5. Product Manager (Non-technical but common for engineers)

Bridge between business and engineering. Requires analytical thinking, user empathy, and stakeholder management.
- Salary: ₹15–60 LPA (mid to senior).
- Often comes from 3–5 years of SDE or consulting background.

## Career Roadmap: Class 12 → Senior SDE

```
Class 12 → JEE/KCET → B.Tech CS/ECE
          ↓
      Internships (Year 2–3) — focus on DSA, projects, open source
          ↓
   Campus Placement (Year 4) — service or product company
          ↓
     SDE-1 (₹6–45 LPA based on company)
          ↓
  Upskill: System Design, Cloud, specialization
          ↓
     SDE-2 → Senior → Lead / Architect / Manager
```

## How to Break Into Product Companies

1. **DSA Preparation:** LeetCode 300+ problems (easy + medium focus). NeetCode 150 is the popular structured list.
2. **Projects:** 2–3 meaningful projects on GitHub with READMEs. Avoid tutorial clones.
3. **Referrals:** 60–70% of product company hires are through referrals. LinkedIn networking matters.
4. **Resume:** 1 page, ATS-friendly, quantify achievements. Use action verbs.

## Top Hiring Companies in India (2024–25)

- **IT Services:** TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL, Cognizant.
- **Indian Product Companies:** Zepto, CRED, Razorpay, PhonePe, Meesho, Groww.
- **MNCs with India offices:** Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Adobe, Oracle, Salesforce.
- **Startups:** Hundreds of funded startups on AngelList India.
