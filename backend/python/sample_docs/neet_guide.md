# NEET UG: Complete Guide for Medical Aspirants

## What is NEET?

**NEET UG (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test)** is the single national-level entrance exam for admission to MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS, BUMS, and allied medical programs in India. It replaced all state-level and private medical entrance tests from 2018. NTA (National Testing Agency) conducts it annually in May.

## Eligibility

- Passed Class 12 with **Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology** as compulsory subjects.
- Minimum **50% aggregate** in PCB (40% for SC/ST/OBC, 45% for PwD).
- Age: Minimum 17 years; no upper age limit (as per Supreme Court 2022 order).
- Number of attempts: Unlimited (as of current rules).

## NEET UG Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Physics | 50 (45 to attempt) | 180 |
| Chemistry | 50 (45 to attempt) | 180 |
| Biology (Botany + Zoology) | 100 (90 to attempt) | 360 |

**Total: 720 marks, 3 hours 20 minutes**
- Each correct answer: +4 marks.
- Each wrong answer: -1 mark.
- Unattempted: 0 marks.

## Section B Strategy

From 2021, each subject has Section A (35 questions, all compulsory) and Section B (15 questions, attempt any 10). Do not attempt all 15 in Section B — attempting wrong answers costs marks.

## Preparation Strategy

### Biology (Most Important — 360 marks)

- NCERT Textbooks (Class 11 & 12) are mandatory. **Every line** in NCERT Biology matters.
- HC Verma equivalent: Trueman's Biology for NEET.
- Focus chapters: Reproduction, Genetics & Evolution, Plant Physiology, Human Physiology.
- Aim for 310+ in Biology to compensate for Physics/Chemistry variance.

### Physics & Chemistry

- NCERT + DC Pandey (Physics), NCERT + N. Avasthi / VK Jaiswal (Chemistry).
- Physics is the differentiator for top ranks — focus on mechanics, electrostatics, optics.
- Organic chemistry: NCERT is sufficient for most reactions; practice named reactions.

### Study Plan

- Daily: 6–8 hours study, 1 mock test per week.
- Revision: 3 full NCERT revisions minimum before the exam.
- PYPs: Solve 10+ years of NEET papers to identify repeat question patterns.

## Seat Allocation

- **15% All India Quota (AIQ)** — managed by MCC (Medical Counselling Committee).
- **85% State Quota** — managed by respective state counselling bodies.
- **Private/Deemed Universities** — separate counselling; high fees (₹5–25 lakhs/year).

## Salary After MBBS

- **Government Doctor (PG after MBBS):** ₹80,000–₹1.5 lakhs/month.
- **Private Hospital (Post-Internship):** ₹30,000–₹60,000/month.
- **Post-MD/MS Specialist:** ₹2–8 lakhs/month in private practice or hospital.

## Alternative Careers for NEET Qualifiers

If MBBS is not secured:
- BAMS (Ayurveda), BHMS (Homeopathy), BUMS (Unani) — lower cutoffs.
- BPharm, B.Sc. Nursing, B.Sc. MLT (Medical Lab Technology).
- Allied Health Sciences (Physiotherapy, Occupational Therapy, Radiology).
