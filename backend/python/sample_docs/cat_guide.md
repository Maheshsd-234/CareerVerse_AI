# CAT 2025: MBA Entrance Exam Complete Guide

## What is CAT?

**CAT (Common Admission Test)** is India's premier MBA entrance exam, conducted annually by one of the **IIMs on a rotation basis**. It is the gateway to **IIMs (Indian Institutes of Management)** and 1,500+ other B-schools across India.

## Eligibility

- Bachelor's degree in any discipline with minimum **50% marks** (45% for SC/ST/PwD).
- Students in final year of graduation can also apply.
- No age limit; no limit on number of attempts.

## CAT Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme

| Section | Questions | Duration |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension (VARC) | 24 | 40 min |
| Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning (DILR) | 20 | 40 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude (QA) | 22 | 40 min |

**Total: 66 questions, 120 minutes**
- MCQ: +3 marks, -1 for wrong answer.
- TITA (Type in the Answer): +3, no negative marking.

## Scoring

- Raw score is **scaled and normalized** across sections and slots.
- **Percentile** not raw score matters — 99+ percentile for top IIMs.
- Section-wise cutoffs also matter (not just overall percentile).

## Preparation Timeline

### 6+ Months Out

- Build fundamentals: Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry (for QA); Grammar, Vocab, RC reading habit (for VARC).
- Books: Arun Sharma (QA), Nishit Sinha (VARC, DILR), How to Prepare for CAT (Agarwal).
- Start reading newspapers/magazines: The Hindu, Business Standard, The Economist.

### 3–6 Months Out

- Enrol in a coaching program or online platform (TIME, IMS, CL, Unacademy CAT).
- Start solving sectional mocks.

### Last 3 Months

- 2–3 full-length mocks per week.
- Deep analysis of every mock: understand WHY each answer is wrong.
- Focus on accuracy over speed.

## Selection Process After CAT

Top IIMs follow a **3-stage process:**
1. CAT Percentile Shortlisting (section-wise + overall cutoffs).
2. Written Ability Test (WAT) — 30-minute essay on a business/social topic.
3. Personal Interview (PI) — 15–30 minutes on academics, work ex, GK, leadership.

**Work Experience:** IIM A/B/C prefer 18–36 months of quality work experience. Fresh graduates are accepted but with lower probability.
